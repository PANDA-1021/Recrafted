Who we are

ReCrafted is a sustainable product brand that focuses on transforming waste materials into useful and attractive products.

We started this business to show that discarded materials still have value and can be turned into something meaningful through creativity and innovation.

Our products combine functionality, affordability and aesthetic designs while promoting the importance of reducing waste.

From waste to worth

Five steps from discarded material to finished product.

1

Collection

Gather discarded materials such as plastic, denim, cardboard and other reusable items.

2

Sorting

Separate materials according to their type and suitability.

3

Preparation

Clean and prepare the materials for reuse.

4

Creation

Transform the materials into useful and attractive products.

5

Finishing

Add final details to improve the appearance and functionality of each product.

Every product represents a new purpose for materials that might otherwise be thrown away.

Our mission

To reduce waste by transforming discarded materials into affordable, practical and creative products while encouraging sustainable lifestyles.

Our vision

To contribute towards a cleaner and greener Maldives by inspiring people to reuse materials, reduce unnecessary waste and make environmentally responsible choices.

ReCrafted transforms waste materials into aesthetic and functional products, combining creativity, affordability and sustainability in one brand.
